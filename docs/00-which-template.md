# Which template should I use?

Tri-Tech Help provides two starter templates. Pick the one that matches your goal.

## website-template (full layout)

**Repo:** [github.com/mdw223/website-template](https://github.com/mdw223/website-template)

**Use when:**
- You want a complete business website quickly
- Your reference sites are standard single-page layouts (hero, services, about, contact)
- You mainly need to change content, colors, and images — not rebuild the whole layout

**Includes:** Hero, services, process steps, portfolio, reviews, about, contact form, light/dark theme.

---

## customizable-website-template (bare shell)

**Repo:** [github.com/mdw223/customizable-website-template](https://github.com/mdw223/customizable-website-template)

**Use when:**
- Your reference websites look very different from a typical business site
- You want AI to rebuild the layout to closely match specific URLs you provide
- You only need working plumbing (contact form, deploy, env vars) and freedom to design everything else

**Includes:** Minimal header/footer, placeholder main section, working Resend contact form, neutral styling.

---

## Quick decision

| Your situation | Choose |
|----------------|--------|
| "I need a business site this week" | **website-template** |
| "I have 3 sites I love and want mine to look like them" | **customizable-website-template** |
| Reference sites are similar to a local service or agency site | **website-template** |
| Reference sites have unusual layouts (video hero, sidebar nav, etc.) | **customizable-website-template** |

Both templates share the same setup guides for GitHub, Vercel, Resend, and Namecheap domains.
